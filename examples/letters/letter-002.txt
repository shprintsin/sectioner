Warsaw, 11 March 1887

To Mr. Jacob Abramson, Vilna

Dear Mr. Abramson,

Thank you for the order of the Reading Society. I have spoken with the printer, Mr. Lewin of Nalewki Street, and he agrees to three roubles and a half a copy for an order of twelve or more.

The books will leave Warsaw on the eighteenth and should reach Vilna within four days. Please let me know when they arrive, and whether the society would also like the second volume, which is promised for the autumn.

Yours faithfully,
Samuel Levin
