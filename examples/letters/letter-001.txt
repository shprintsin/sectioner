Vilna, 3 March 1887

To Mr. Samuel Levin, bookseller, Warsaw

Dear Sir,

I received your letter of the twentieth of February together with the catalogue of the new books. The Reading Society has decided to order twelve copies of the history of the Jewish communities of Lithuania, and asks that they be sent by rail before the end of the month.

The price you name, four roubles a copy, seems to us high. Mr. Abramson, our treasurer, reminds me that last year the same printer sold to the society in Kovno at three roubles and a half. We would be grateful if you could match that price.

With respect,
Jacob Abramson, Secretary of the Reading Society
